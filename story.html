Our story

Designed like a Swiss watch.

How a scattered makeup bag turned into a click-shut case.

The beginning

Worn like a second skin.

Miss Swiss started with a simple frustration: brush sets that scatter, bristles that shed, cases that crack in a handbag. So we rebuilt the compact from the ground up — a clear acrylic shell that snaps shut, cradling every brush in its own gold-lined sleeve.

Every bristle is cut and rounded by hand, then set in a resin ferrule instead of glued — so a Miss Swiss brush still blends clean after two hundred washes. No animal hair, ever.
